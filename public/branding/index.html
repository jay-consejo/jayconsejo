JDC — Brand Guidelines

The monogram, the palette, the typography, and how to use them. Serves as the source of truth for email signatures, social avatars, and marketing collateral.

01The mark

The monogram JDC set in Playfair Display Semibold with tight tracking. Serif for craft; warm gold for warmth over corporate steel. Two shapes × two schemes.

email avatar, social bio
circle · warm light
letterhead, print

business card, doc cover

04Clear space & minimum size

Keep at least 12% of the mark's width of empty room on every side. Minimum display size: 48px for the circle, 56px for the square. Below that, switch to the "JDC" wordmark in text.

clear space · 12%

05Do & Don't

Do

  • Use gold as accent, never as the dominant background fill.
  • Pair dark mark with dark bg, light mark with cream bg.
  • Keep the serif — swap to Inter only in body copy.
  • Leave 12% clear space minimum on all sides.
  • Prefer PNG for email, SVG for web.

Don't

  • Don't add gradients, glows, drop shadows, or 3D effects.
  • Don't add decorative rings, frames, or borders.
  • Don't stretch, skew, or recolor the mark.
  • Don't place the mark on busy photography without a scrim.
  • Don't use below 48px (circle) or 56px (square).
  • Don't substitute the serif. If Playfair isn't available, use the PNG.
