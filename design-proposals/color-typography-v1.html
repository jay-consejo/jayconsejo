Professional Recommendation

I'd go with Option A — Obsidian Luxe

Here's why: The Playfair Display + Inter pairing strikes the perfect balance between "wow" and usability. The serif display font creates an immediate sense of prestige and craftsmanship, while Inter keeps body text crisp and scannable. It doesn't try too hard — it's confident.

The warm gold accent (#C8A87E) against the deep dark background creates a visual hierarchy that draws the eye naturally. Gold communicates quality without feeling loud or corporate. It's the difference between a luxury brand and a tech startup — this palette says "I take my craft seriously."

That said, Option C (Ember & Silk) is a strong runner-up if you want more energy and personality. The ember accent is bolder and more memorable — great if you want visitors to feel an immediate spark. Instrument Serif is also having a moment in design right now.

Option B (Electric Slate) is a great tech-forward choice but leans more "developer portfolio" than "premium creative." Best if your primary audience is other developers or tech companies.

All options support dark/light modes, meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements, and use a 4px spacing grid. Typography scales responsively across all breakpoints.
