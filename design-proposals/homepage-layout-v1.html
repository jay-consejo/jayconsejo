My Recommendation

Option C — The Narrative

Here's my reasoning: Your target audience — startup founders searching for an "AI development partner" and freelancers wanting to automate — are people who've been burned before. They've talked to agencies, scrolled through generic portfolios, and gotten pitch decks that led nowhere. What they're looking for is a real person who gets it.

The narrative layout opens with "Hey, I'm Jay" and immediately establishes you as a human, not a template. The process section answers their #1 question ("how do you actually work?") before they ask. And the case-study approach to projects is far more persuasive than thumbnails — because founders care about the story of the problem solved, not just the screenshot.

The "limited availability" CTA creates subtle urgency — it says "I'm in demand but I'm choosing to talk to you," which is exactly the premium, solution-driven feel you described. This layout also matches the Obsidian Luxe palette perfectly — the dark background with warm gold accents gives the narrative room to breathe and feel elevated.

That said, I'd steal two elements from the other options:

From Option A: Add a small stats bar (3-4 key numbers) right after the hero. Even in a narrative flow, numbers build instant credibility.

From Option B: Include a skills pill section near the about area. Founders often ctrl+F for specific technologies.

Bottom line: Option C best delivers the feeling you described — "this person is serious, creative, solution-driven, and always makes things happen" — while staying far from corporate templates or overly minimal designs.
