## Recommendation Section

Styled as a premium card: gradient background #111114 to #161620 at 135deg, 1px solid #222 border, 24px border-radius, 56px 48px padding, 96px top margin. Has a decorative ::before pseudo-element — a 1px horizontal line with gradient from transparent to #555 and back.

**Label:** "Professional Recommendation" at 11px, color #666

**Title:** "I'd go with Option A — Obsidian Luxe" — Playfair Display, 32px, weight 400

**Reasoning (4 paragraphs):**

1. Playfair Display + Inter is the ideal balance of "wow" and usability. Serif creates prestige, Inter keeps body text scannable. "Confident without trying too hard."

2. The gold accent #C8A87E against deep dark creates natural visual hierarchy. "Gold communicates quality without feeling loud or corporate... this palette says 'I take my craft seriously.'"

3. Option C (Ember & Silk) called out as a strong runner-up — bolder, more energetic. Instrument Serif noted as trending in current design.

4. Option B (Electric Slate) positioned as best for a tech/developer audience, but leans more "developer portfolio" than "premium creative."

**Footer note** (13px, color #666): All options support dark/light modes, meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, use 4px spacing grid, and responsive typography.
